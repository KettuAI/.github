## KettuAI

We build Kettu, one home for all your coding agents, on your computer, your phone and in the browser.

What we care about:

- **Beauty is productivity.** We design every screen with care, down to the motion and the type, because you work better in a tool you enjoy.
- **Your agents, your choice.** Kettu works with the agents you already use, Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and 40+ more, with your own accounts and settings. No lock-in to one vendor.
- **Your work stays yours.** Your sessions, code and files stay on your own computers, and the relay between your devices cannot read them.
- **Built in the open.** Kettu is open source under AGPL-3.0 and shaped by the people who use it.
